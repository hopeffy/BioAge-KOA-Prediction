----------------------------------------

  sbp.mean is in original scale (mmHg)

4. ENCODING MAPPINGS
----------------------------------------
  Gender:       1=Male, 2=Female
  Age_New:      1=younger(45-59), 2=older(60+)
  BMI_New:      1=underweight/normal, 2=overweight, 3=obese
  Marital:      1=married, 2=other
  Education:    1=low, 2=medium, 3=high
  Residence:    1=urban, 2=rural
  Disease cols: 0=no, 1=yes (Hypertension, Dyslipidemia, Diabetes, Cancer, CVD)
  Smoke:        0=never, 1=ever
  Drink:        0=never, 1=ever
  KOA:          0=no, 1=yes (Arthritis=yes AND position_knees=yes)

5. KOA DEFINITION
----------------------------------------
  KOA = (Arthritis=='yes') AND (position_knees=='yes')
  This matches the paper's 'Symptomatic KOA' definition
  Verification: 100% match between KOA and (Arthritis AND position_knees)

6. LEAKAGE CONTROL
----------------------------------------

  Reason: KOA is directly derived from these columns
