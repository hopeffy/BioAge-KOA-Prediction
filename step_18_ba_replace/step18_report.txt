PURPOSE
----------------------------------------
Replace Sheet1's opaque 'Biological Age' with transparent KDM-BA
and PhenoAge_Adapted inside raw17. Same feature count, same CV.
Tests whether Sheet1 BA is irreplaceable or any biomarker-derived
BA achieves equivalent performance.

METHODOLOGY
----------------------------------------

  CV: 3 seeds (42,123,999) x 5-fold StratifiedKFold = 15 runs each
  Standardization: StandardScaler (fit on train only)
  Metrics: AUROC, PR-AUC, Brier, F1
