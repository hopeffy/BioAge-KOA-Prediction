\section{Validation and Leakage Controls}
\begin{itemize}
\item Early 80/20 stratified split generated before active model development in Step 01.
\item Active pipeline uses train-fitted imputation only (SimpleImputer median/mode).
\item Internal discrimination and calibration are reported using OOF predictions.
\item Unseen holdout metrics are reported side-by-side with internal OOF summaries.
\end{itemize}

\section{Legacy and Exploratory Branches}
Legacy branch scripts and prior optimization tracks are preserved for reproducibility and auditability, but they are not used as primary evidence in the main manuscript claim structure.
